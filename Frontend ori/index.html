      MODIS → VIIRS calibration

      Each dot is one month in which both sensors observed (Feb 2012–2025). The line is the fitted model used to bring older MODIS months onto the VIIRS scale.

      Method. Every hotspot is snapped to a 0.05° grid. A cell that burns on a given day counts once, as one fire cell-day, however many pixels detect it. This evens out the resolution gap between VIIRS (375 m) and MODIS (1 km). For each region, either a linear or a power-law model is fitted on the overlap months. It is validated by leaving out one year at a time, and the model with the lower error is kept. "vs typical" compares each month with the median of the same calendar month across 2003–2025.

      Hotspot data: NASA FIRMS (MODIS C6.1, VIIRS S-NPP 375 m). Basemap © Esri, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
